**Found during the EPUB build (not a reader finding)**

- **The book promised "fillable fields in the digital edition" that don't exist.** A standard EPUB has no fillable fields, and e-readers can't reliably keep form entries. Worse, the Privacy note said "nothing you type is saved", but Kindle and other e-readers sync the reader's own notes to their accounts.
- **What changed:** the Privacy note, the Introduction's worksheet note, and the C4 intro now tell the truth.
  - The book stores nothing.
  - Worksheets are for paper or a private notes app.
  - The e-reader's own note features may sync to its maker.
- **Effect on the pass 1 fixes:** this removes the Privacy-vs-Safety-Plan contradiction at its root, so condition 4's "your entries will be wiped" warning is no longer needed. Conflict 6's privacy guidance is kept.
